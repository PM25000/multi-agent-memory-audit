# Public exclusions

The public copies omit manuscript and historical draft contents, publisher
files, original answer-sheet exports, internal author/submission records,
whole virtual environments, tokenizer binaries, temporary locks, obsolete
prelive datasets, duplicate frozen-source copies and old build/projection files.
The original local ZIPs remain unchanged.

The aborted predecessor of the S6 token extension is not part of the final
study's effects or public raw outcomes. EXCLUSIONS.json retains aggregate
accounting and the reason for exclusion: 157 recorded provider receipts and
four unresolved intents, after a dependency implementation failure. Its legacy
software_aborted_pilot field is an archival label, not evidence of a prospectively
planned pilot. The final v2 study retains all 120 named/90 unique positions.

Current-study failures and negative results remain in S1-S8. S5 removes two
pilot fixtures while retaining all twelve analyzed source-audit workflows.
S7 retains the actual simple-rule zero-additional-decision result, tradeoff
screen and repeated native SDK outcomes. No observation was dropped merely
because its result was unfavorable.

Public manifests record retained original byte identities and documented
transformations. New public dependency gates do not assert that excluded
historical manuscript/environment gates passed.

## Historical indices and current public members

Historical freeze manifests and upstream source indices retain necessary file
paths and hashes for provenance, including references to excluded manuscripts,
prelive revisions, environments and upstream development files. Those file
contents are not distributed. Each package's PUBLIC_MANIFEST.json defines
the current public members. Historical indices do not assert that old
full-tree, manuscript or environment gates passed.
